## About Me

I'm a **Webflow Developer, Software Engineer, and SEO Specialist** from Dhaka, Bangladesh, passionate about building modern websites, developing software solutions, and improving digital experiences.

I enjoy combining design, development, and search optimization to create websites that are not only visually appealing but also functional, accessible, and performance-focused.

Beyond my current work, I'm continuously expanding my technical knowledge in software engineering, computer systems, and networking, with a long-term ambition to build a career in **Cybersecurity**.

My ultimate goal is to become a skilled cybersecurity professional capable of understanding, building, and protecting secure digital systems.

- **Working On:** Webflow development, websites & SEO.
- **Exploring:** Software engineering and modern web technologies.
- **Interested In:** Website performance, technical SEO & digital growth.
- **Long-Term Goal:** Building a career in cybersecurity and digital security.
